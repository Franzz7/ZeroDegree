Water Care &
Safe Use Guide

For Home Hire, Events, Gym Hire and Productions · Last updated: 12 August 2026

This guide should be read with the applicable Home Hire Terms & Conditions or Event, Gym, Promotional & Production Hire Terms.

See also: Home Hire Terms & Conditions · Event, Gym, Promotional & Production Hire Terms

1. Responsibilities

Deep Chill will deliver and install the agreed equipment, check the initial setup and explain its basic operation.

After handover:

  • self-managed home-hire customers are responsible for day-to-day water care, cleaning, monitoring and safe use in accordance with this guide and Deep Chill’s instructions;
  • fully managed home-hire customers must monitor the equipment and follow Deep Chill’s instructions between service visits;
  • gyms, venues and event organisers are responsible for daily operation, water care, testing, cleaning, supervision and safe use; and
  • production clients are responsible for the equipment and location during the agreed hire period.

Where an Ozone Unit is supplied, it helps support water quality between cleans or service visits. It does not replace cleaning, filtration, water testing, the agreed sanitiser procedure or any responsibility in this guide.

Nothing in this guide removes any responsibility Deep Chill has under applicable law as equipment owner, supplier or installer.

2. General Water Checks

For shared-use setups, including gyms, venues and events where water testing is required, check:

  • bromine;
  • pH;
  • water clarity and smell;
  • visible dirt or contamination;
  • circulation and water flow; and
  • the chiller, hoses and connections.

Unless Deep Chill confirms otherwise in writing, the operating ranges for an agreed shared-use setup are:

  • Bromine: 4–6 mg/L
  • pH: 7.2–7.6

Follow the treatment-product label and Deep Chill’s instructions. Do not guess chemical quantities or mix treatment products.

Where a floating bromine dispenser is used, remove it before anyone enters the plunge and return it afterwards as instructed.

For home hire, follow the water-care routine provided by Deep Chill for the selected package. Do not add bromine, chlorine, bleach, salts, cleaners, oils, fragrances, dyes or other substances unless Deep Chill has specifically instructed you to do so.

3. Home Hire

Self-Managed Home Hire

Self-managed home-hire customers are responsible for day-to-day water care and cleaning throughout the hire period.

The customer must:

  • keep the tub covered when not in use;
  • operate the chiller and circulation as instructed;
  • remove visible debris;
  • check the water’s appearance and smell;
  • keep the tub and surrounding area clean;
  • rinse or shower before use where practical;
  • avoid introducing dirt, oils, lotions or fake tan;
  • follow the water-refresh and cleaning routine provided by Deep Chill; and
  • stop use and contact Deep Chill if there is any concern about the water or equipment.

Self-Managed + Ozone Unit customers must also operate the Ozone Unit only as instructed. The Ozone Unit helps support water quality between cleans, but it does not guarantee that water will remain suitable for use or replace cleaning, filtration or the required water-care routine.

Deep Chill does not carry out scheduled service visits for self-managed hire unless separately agreed in writing.

Fully Managed Home Hire

Deep Chill normally services Fully Managed Home Hire equipment every 14 days. A service may include cleaning the tub, refreshing or replacing the water, checking the filter, chiller, circulation, hoses and connections, and checking for leaks or faults.

Between visits, the customer must:

  • keep the tub covered when not in use;
  • operate the chiller and circulation as instructed;
  • remove visible debris;
  • check the water’s appearance and smell;
  • rinse or shower before use where practical; and
  • avoid introducing dirt, oils, lotions or fake tan.

Fully managed home-hire customers must not add bromine, chlorine, bleach, salts, cleaners, oils, fragrances, dyes or other treatment products unless specifically instructed by Deep Chill.

The 14-day service interval is not a guarantee that the water will remain suitable for exactly 14 days. Stop use and contact Deep Chill if there is any concern.

4. Managed Gym Hire

The gym must appoint a responsible person to manage the plunge and keep appropriate records.

Where fitted, the Ozone Unit provides ozone water-care support between Deep Chill service visits. It does not replace the gym’s responsibility for daily water testing, bromine and pH management, sanitiser procedure, cleaning, filter care, supervision or record keeping.

The gym must:

  • test bromine and pH before opening and during use as required;
  • monitor water clarity, smell, circulation and contamination;
  • clean the tub and waterline regularly;
  • remove visible debris;
  • rinse the filter at every clean;
  • maintain the correct water level;
  • follow the agreed sanitiser procedure;
  • control access and supervise use; and
  • close the plunge whenever safe operation or water quality is uncertain.

Water-change frequency will depend on usage, test results and water condition. Deep Chill will carry out scheduled service checks approximately every two to three weeks, but the gym remains responsible for daily care between visits.

5. Event Hire

Deep Chill will provide the agreed fresh-water setup and initial checks before handover.

The organiser must:

  • appoint a responsible person;
  • supervise access throughout the event;
  • carry out any testing or water-care steps explained at handover;
  • keep the surrounding area clear and safe;
  • prevent contamination and misuse; and
  • stop use if readings or conditions become unsuitable.

Unless on-site staffing has been agreed, Deep Chill is not required to remain after handover.

6. Production Hire

Where the equipment is supplied only as a photographic or filming prop, nobody may enter the tub unless immersion has been agreed with Deep Chill.

Where people will enter the water, the event-hire water-care and safe-use rules apply. The production organiser is responsible for supervision, access control and site safety after handover.

7. Safe-Use Rules

Users must:

  • enter and exit carefully;
  • keep their head above water;
  • breathe normally;
  • use sensible immersion times;
  • follow the responsible person’s instructions; and
  • leave immediately if they feel unwell.

Users must not:

  • dive or jump in;
  • deliberately hyperventilate or hold their breath underwater;
  • use the plunge while intoxicated;
  • bring food, drinks or chewing gum into the plunge;
  • add anything to the water; or
  • use the plunge if they cannot enter and exit safely.

Anyone uncertain whether cold-water immersion is suitable for them should obtain medical advice. This is particularly important for people with heart, blood-pressure or breathing conditions, a history of fainting, pregnancy, relevant medication or other health concerns.

Anyone under 18 may use the equipment only where Deep Chill has agreed this in writing, the equipment manufacturer permits it, and appropriate direct adult supervision is provided.

8. Stop Use Immediately

Close the plunge and contact Deep Chill if:

  • bromine or pH is outside the agreed range;
  • water is cloudy, dirty or smells unusual;
  • circulation or the chiller stops working normally;
  • there is a significant leak or electrical concern;
  • a hose, fitting, cable or plug is damaged;
  • there has been vomit, faecal matter, significant blood or unknown chemical contamination;
  • users are not following the rules;
  • the surrounding area or weather conditions are unsafe; or
  • there is any doubt about continued use.

9. Equipment and Electrical Safety

Do not open, repair, move or modify the chiller or other equipment without Deep Chill’s permission.

Do not use damaged plugs, cables or adapters, or an extension lead unless agreed by Deep Chill.

Stop use immediately if there is electrical tripping, smoke, a burning smell, unusual electrical behaviour or water entering an electrical component.

Outdoor equipment must not be used in unsuitable weather and should be protected from rain as instructed.

10. Cleaning and Records

Use only products suitable for the equipment and approved or specified by Deep Chill.

Gyms and other shared-use establishments should record:

  • water-test results;
  • cleaning and filter rinsing;
  • water changes;
  • chemical adjustments;
  • faults and contamination incidents; and
  • any period when the plunge was closed.

11. Contact Deep Chill

Contact Deep Chill about equipment faults, leaks, loss of circulation, contamination or uncertainty about continued use.

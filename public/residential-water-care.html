Residential Water Care &
Safe Use Guide

For private residential customers · Last updated: 09/07/2026

This guide explains how to look after your Deep Chill cold plunge between scheduled maintenance visits, how to help protect the water quality, and how to use the plunge safely.

Deep Chill provides a managed residential service. We normally visit approximately every 10–14 days to inspect, clean and maintain the equipment and refresh or replace the water where appropriate.

The MyoChill used in Deep Chill installations circulates the water and uses a built-in micron particle filter to remove debris such as hair, leaves, dust, sediment and sand. The standard MyoChill is a filtration and chilling system; MyoMaster describes ozone disinfection separately in its MyoChill+ and MyoZone products.

This residential guide is designed for private home use, where the plunge is used by the customer, their household and occasional personal guests. It is not intended for commercial gyms, public events or high-volume shared facilities.

1. The Simple Rules

To help keep your plunge clean and in good condition:

Before you plunge:

  • check that the water looks and smells normal
  • shower or rinse first where reasonably practical
  • avoid using the plunge while covered in excessive oils, lotions, creams or fake tan
  • remove excessive dirt, grass or mud before getting in

After you plunge:

  • remove any obvious leaves, grass or floating debris
  • replace the cover
  • leave the equipment operating as instructed by Deep Chill
  • tell us if you notice reduced water flow, a leak or anything unusual

Do not add anything to the water.

Unless specifically instructed by Deep Chill, do not add:

  • chlorine
  • bromine
  • bleach
  • disinfectant
  • cleaning products
  • bath salts
  • Epsom salts
  • essential oils
  • soap
  • bubble bath
  • fragrances
  • dyes
  • any other water-treatment product

2. Before Every Use

Before entering the plunge, take a few seconds to check the water and equipment.

Check the water

The water should:

  • look normal and reasonably clear
  • have no unusual or concerning smell
  • contain no obvious significant contamination

Do not use the plunge if:

  • the water is significantly cloudy
  • the water smells unusual or unpleasant
  • someone has vomited in the water
  • there has been faecal contamination
  • an unknown chemical or substance has been added
  • you have another reason to believe the water has been significantly contaminated

Contact Deep Chill if you are unsure.

Cold water does not sterilise itself. PWTAG notes that microorganisms can survive in cold-water systems where there is no effective disinfection, which is why good hygiene, filtration, proper maintenance and appropriate water replacement remain important. PWTAG’s main ice-bath guidance focuses on commercial settings but says domestic users should follow the guidance as closely as reasonably possible.

3. Shower or Rinse Before Use

Where reasonably practical, shower or rinse before entering the plunge.

This is particularly important if you have:

  • just exercised heavily
  • been running on muddy or grassy ground
  • used body oil
  • used moisturising cream
  • applied fake tan
  • been wearing heavy makeup
  • been gardening
  • been doing another activity that may introduce significant dirt or contaminants into the water

PWTAG’s ice-bath guidance recommends pre-immersion hygiene because users introduce contaminants into shared bath water.

For a private home plunge, we are not asking you to have a full shower every single time regardless of circumstances. The aim is simple: avoid carrying unnecessary dirt, sweat and body products into the water.

4. Keep Body Products Out of the Water

Body products can affect the appearance and condition of the water and place additional demand on filtration and maintenance.

Try to avoid entering the plunge while covered in excessive:

  • fake tan
  • body oil
  • massage oil
  • moisturiser
  • sun cream
  • heavy makeup
  • other oily or cosmetic products

A small amount remaining on normal skin does not mean the plunge must automatically be closed or the water replaced. The aim is to reduce unnecessary build-up.

Where practical, rinse first.

5. Keep the Tub Covered

Replace the cover after use.

Keeping the tub covered helps reduce the amount of:

  • leaves
  • grass
  • insects
  • dust
  • animal debris
  • other outdoor material

entering the water.

The cover should be fitted securely but should not be used as a seat, table or play surface.

Do not place heavy objects on the cover.

6. Leaves and Visible Debris

Small amounts of outdoor debris are normal, particularly where a plunge is located in a garden.

Between Deep Chill visits, you may remove obvious floating debris such as:

  • leaves
  • grass
  • small insects
  • other loose material

Do not dismantle the filter system or open the chiller to deal with debris.

The MyoChill’s built-in particle filter is designed to capture physical contaminants including hair, leaves, dust, sediment and sand.

Deep Chill will deal with filter inspection and replacement as part of the managed maintenance service unless we specifically agree otherwise with you.

7. Do Not Switch Off or Change the System Without Checking With Us

Operate the chiller and circulation system in the way explained by Deep Chill at installation.

Please do not:

  • repeatedly switch the system on and off without reason
  • change hose arrangements
  • disconnect hoses
  • move the chiller
  • open the chiller casing
  • remove fittings
  • attempt repairs
  • change the installation setup

without speaking to Deep Chill first.

The system depends on appropriate water flow and correct connection of the tub, hoses, pump, filter and chiller.

Contact us if you need to move the equipment or temporarily disconnect it.

8. Water Level

Keep an eye on the water level.

Some water loss is normal through:

  • getting in and out
  • splashing
  • water carried out on the body or clothing
  • normal use

Contact Deep Chill if:

  • the water level drops unexpectedly
  • you find water around the equipment that is not explained by normal splashing
  • a hose appears to be leaking
  • a fitting is dripping continuously
  • the tub appears to be losing water

Do not ignore a significant or unexplained drop in water level.

9. Water Flow

The MyoChill system relies on water circulating through the system.

Contact Deep Chill if you notice:

  • significantly reduced flow
  • no visible or noticeable circulation where circulation is normally present
  • unusual noises from the pump or chiller
  • repeated stopping and starting
  • leaking hoses or fittings
  • an error or fault with the chiller

Do not dismantle the equipment or attempt to repair it yourself.

10. Deep Chill Maintenance Visits

Deep Chill normally visits approximately every 10–14 days.

A maintenance visit may include:

  • checking the tub
  • inspecting the chiller
  • checking hoses and fittings
  • checking water flow and circulation
  • inspecting the filtration system
  • replacing or servicing the filter where appropriate
  • cleaning the tub
  • refreshing or replacing water where appropriate
  • checking for visible faults, leaks or maintenance issues

MyoMaster currently states that its MyoChill replacement filter interval is usage-dependent, giving ranges of approximately 4–8 weeks on the MyoChill product page and 30–60 days on the replacement-filter page. Deep Chill therefore manages filter replacement according to use, condition and the requirements of the particular installation rather than asking residential customers to follow a fixed DIY schedule.

Important

The normal 10–14 day Deep Chill maintenance interval does not mean that every body of water is guaranteed to remain suitable for exactly 10 or 14 days.

Water condition varies depending on:

  • frequency of use
  • number of users
  • whether users rinse first
  • body products
  • outdoor debris
  • weather
  • contamination
  • the operation of the equipment

Contact us early if the water condition changes.

You do not need to wait until the next scheduled visit.

11. When to Contact Deep Chill Before the Next Visit

Please contact us if:

  • the water becomes significantly cloudy
  • the water develops an unusual smell
  • there is significant visible contamination
  • water circulation appears to reduce substantially
  • the chiller stops working
  • the pump makes an unusual noise
  • the water level drops unexpectedly
  • a hose or fitting is leaking
  • an electrical problem is suspected
  • someone adds an unknown product or chemical to the water
  • someone vomits in the plunge
  • there is faecal contamination
  • there is significant blood contamination
  • you are unsure whether the plunge should continue to be used

The safest simple rule is:

When in doubt, stop using the plunge and contact Deep Chill.

12. Significant Contamination

Stop using the plunge immediately and contact Deep Chill following significant contamination.

Examples include:

  • faecal contamination
  • vomiting in the water
  • a substantial amount of blood
  • an unknown chemical or cleaning product entering the water
  • another significant contamination event

Do not attempt to solve a contamination problem by adding bleach, chlorine or another disinfectant yourself.

The correct response may involve draining, cleaning, filter attention and refilling, depending on what has happened.

13. Personal Hygiene and Illness

Do not use the plunge if you currently have vomiting or diarrhoea.

PWTAG’s ice-bath guidance advises that people who have had sickness or diarrhoea should wait at least 48 hours after symptoms have stopped before using an ice bath, with longer exclusion guidance applying in some specific diagnosed infections such as cryptosporidiosis.

You should also avoid using the plunge where:

  • you have a significant open wound that could contaminate the water
  • you are acutely unwell
  • you are affected by alcohol or recreational drugs
  • you are unable to enter or exit safely

Covering a small minor cut with a suitable waterproof dressing may be appropriate, but where there is a significant open wound or uncertainty, wait until it has healed or seek appropriate advice.

14. Basic Cold Plunge Safety

Cold water immersion affects the body quickly and should be approached sensibly.

Enter slowly and carefully

Do not jump or dive into the plunge.

Enter in a controlled way and allow yourself time to adjust to the cold.

Keep your breathing controlled

Do not deliberately hyperventilate or perform prolonged breath-holding exercises in or immediately before entering the water.

Never practise underwater breath-holding in the plunge.

Keep your head above water

Do not deliberately submerge your head.

PWTAG’s ice-bath guidance advises users not to put their head under the water and not to ingest the water.

Know when to get out

Leave the water immediately if you feel:

  • dizzy
  • faint
  • confused
  • unusually breathless
  • severely uncomfortable
  • unable to control your breathing
  • excessively numb
  • disorientated
  • otherwise unwell

Do not treat a cold plunge as an endurance competition.

Longer is not automatically better.

15. Alcohol and Recreational Drugs

Do not use the cold plunge while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs.

They may impair:

  • judgement
  • balance
  • awareness
  • safe entry and exit
  • the ability to recognise when something is wrong

Wait until you are fully sober and able to make safe decisions.

16. Medical Suitability

Cold water immersion may not be suitable for everyone.

Deep Chill does not provide medical advice.

Seek appropriate medical advice before using cold water immersion if you:

  • have a heart condition
  • have high or low blood pressure
  • have breathing difficulties
  • have a history of fainting
  • are pregnant
  • take medication that may be relevant to cold exposure
  • have a medical condition that may be affected by cold exposure
  • are unsure whether cold water immersion is suitable for you

Do not use the plunge simply because another person says that it is safe for their condition.

Individual circumstances differ.

17. Children and Young People

No person under the age of 18 should use the plunge without appropriate direct adult supervision.

The supervising adult must:

  • remain present
  • ensure safe entry and exit
  • control the length of the session appropriately
  • watch for signs of distress
  • prevent diving, jumping and horseplay
  • make sure the young person can get out promptly

The plunge should not be treated as a paddling pool or play area.

Keep the cover secured when the plunge is not in use.

18. Guests

You may allow occasional personal guests to use the plunge as part of normal private home use.

Before they use it, make sure they understand:

  • how to enter safely
  • not to dive or jump
  • not to put their head underwater
  • not to use it while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs
  • to get out immediately if they feel unwell
  • the need to consider their own medical suitability
  • the basic hygiene rules in this guide

Do not charge people to use the plunge or use it for commercial sessions under a residential Deep Chill agreement.

19. Safe Entry and Exit

Take care when entering and leaving the plunge.

Water around the tub can make surfaces slippery.

Where necessary:

  • use the step provided or agreed for the installation
  • hold a stable support where one is available
  • step carefully
  • avoid rushing
  • keep the area clear of unnecessary obstacles
  • keep towels and personal belongings out of the walking path

Do not use unstable furniture, boxes or improvised objects as steps.

20. Keep the Area Around the Plunge Safe

The customer should take reasonable steps to keep the area around the plunge:

  • free from trip hazards
  • clear of loose electrical cables
  • reasonably free from pooled water
  • accessible for safe entry and exit
  • accessible to Deep Chill for maintenance

Tell us if changes to the garden, decking, patio or surrounding area affect the installation.

21. Electrical Safety

Do not use the equipment where an electrical safety concern is suspected.

Contact Deep Chill if you notice:

  • repeated electrical tripping
  • damage to the plug or cable
  • a burning smell
  • smoke
  • unusual electrical behaviour
  • water entering electrical components
  • another electrical concern

Do not:

  • open the chiller
  • modify electrical cables
  • attempt electrical repairs
  • use damaged electrical equipment
  • use extension leads unless agreed with Deep Chill

Stop use and contact us where there is any genuine electrical concern.

22. Pets and Animals

Do not allow pets to use the plunge.

Take reasonable steps to prevent pets from:

  • entering the water
  • damaging hoses
  • chewing cables
  • scratching or damaging the tub
  • contaminating the water

Contact Deep Chill if an animal significantly contaminates the water.

23. What Not to Put in the Plunge

Unless Deep Chill specifically approves it, do not put any of the following in the water:

  • ice containing additives
  • salt
  • Epsom salts
  • bath bombs
  • essential oils
  • bubble bath
  • soap
  • shampoo
  • bath products
  • perfume
  • dyes
  • cleaning chemicals
  • chlorine
  • bromine
  • bleach
  • pool chemicals
  • spa chemicals
  • homemade water treatments
  • any unknown substance

The standard MyoChill provides chilling, circulation and micron particle filtration; MyoMaster describes ozone cleaning or disinfection as separate functionality in MyoZone and MyoChill+ products.

Deep Chill is currently reviewing additional water-treatment options for different types of installations. Do not introduce a treatment product to a Deep Chill system unless we have approved it for that installation.

24. What Deep Chill Looks After

As part of your managed residential rental, Deep Chill is responsible for the scheduled servicing and maintenance activities included in your arrangement.

Deep Chill will normally manage:

  • routine equipment inspection
  • filter inspection and replacement where appropriate
  • tub cleaning during scheduled maintenance
  • water refresh or replacement where appropriate
  • hose and fitting checks
  • circulation checks
  • fault investigation
  • planned maintenance
  • collection when the rental ends

The customer is not expected to become a pool technician.

Your role between visits is simply to:

  • use the plunge responsibly
  • keep unnecessary contamination out
  • keep it covered
  • watch for obvious changes
  • report problems early

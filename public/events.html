Cold Plunge Hire for Gyms

Give your members access to cold-water recovery without the upfront cost of buying equipment or the responsibility of maintaining it yourself.

Managed Gym Hire

Three-month minimum hire. No deposit.

£250per month

Included

  • One cold-plunge tub with chiller and filtration
  • Ozone unit for water-care support, helping support water quality between service visits
  • Delivery and professional setup
  • Scheduled service visit, deep clean and equipment check every two to three weeks
  • Inspection and cleaning of the tub, chiller, filter, hoses and connections
  • Filter replacement or change where required
  • Equipment-fault support, including repair or replacement equipment where required
  • Collection at the end of the hire period

The ozone unit helps support water quality between service visits. It does not replace filtration, cleaning, water testing or the agreed sanitiser procedure.

The gym remains responsible for daily cleaning, water testing, bromine and pH management, sanitiser procedure, access control, supervision, record keeping and safe member use between Deep Chill service visits.

A suitable installation area, power supply, water supply and drainage are required.

Delivery, setup, scheduled servicing and collection are included within 15 miles of Horley. Additional travel charges may apply outside this area.

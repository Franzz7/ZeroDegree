Become a Deep Chill Partner

We partner with selected gyms, fitness studios, sports clubs and wellness businesses that share our belief that recovery is just as important as training.

By working together, we help members take their recovery beyond the gym or wherever they train with convenient, premium at-home cold plunge hire.

Deep Chill partners may:

  • Be featured on our Partners page
  • Share Deep Chill’s at-home cold plunge hire with their members or clients where agreed
  • Receive selected promotional event-hire rates
  • Collaborate on social-media content and local events
  • Take part in referral or partner offers agreed with Deep Chill

Partnership and promotional-hire arrangements are agreed individually and are subject to availability. Promotional event-hire rates apply only where the agreed activity and promotion have been confirmed in advance.

From your first enquiry to delivery, servicing and final collection, we keep the process clear and straightforward.

Home Hire

Self-managed or fully managed, your choice.

We deliver and install your cold plunge. From there, choose Self-Managed and look after the water care yourself, add MyoZone for extra support, or go Fully Managed and let us handle it with a service visit every 14 days.

Enquire

Complete our short enquiry form and tell us about your home and preferred installation area. There is no commitment at this stage.

We confirm suitability

We check your location, access, available space, power supply, water supply and drainage. We then confirm availability and answer any questions before the hire begins.

Delivery and installation

We deliver and professionally install the cold plunge tub and chiller. We position the equipment, connect the system, prepare it for use and explain how everything works before we leave.

Self-managed or fully managed

With Self-Managed, you handle day-to-day water care yourself, following our simple routine (add MyoZone for extra support). With Fully Managed, we visit every 14 days to clean the tub, maintain the water and inspect the chiller, filter, hoses and connections. You do not usually need to be home, provided safe access has been arranged.

When you decide to end the hire after the minimum term, the required notice period applies and we arrange collection of the equipment.

Event Hire

A professionally managed setup for your event.

Suitable for gym open days, corporate wellness events, sports events, private occasions and agreed promotional partner events.

Tell us about your event

Send us the proposed date, venue, event times and expected use. We will confirm availability and whether promotional or standard event hire is appropriate.

We confirm the arrangements

We confirm the price, delivery and collection times, access requirements and practical details before the booking goes ahead. A suitable installation area, power supply, water supply and drainage are required.

Delivery and professional setup

We deliver the cold plunge tub and chiller, complete the fresh-water setup and prepare the equipment for use. We also provide the organiser with safe-use and water-checking guidance.

Event use and collection

The organiser supervises the equipment and participants during the event unless on-site support has been agreed separately. We return at the agreed time to drain, disconnect and collect the equipment.

Standard event hire includes up to five hours of event use. Additional charges may apply for extra mileage, ice, overnight cooling, extended hire, out-of-hours attendance or more complex installation requirements.

Managed Gym Hire

Ongoing cold plunge access for your members.

We supply, install and regularly service a cold plunge for your gym, while your team manages the agreed daily checks and safe use between visits.

Tell us about your gym

Complete the enquiry form with your gym’s location, proposed installation area, expected usage and available facilities.

We confirm site suitability

We confirm access, space, flooring protection, ventilation, power, water and drainage requirements. We also explain the gym’s daily cleaning, water-testing and member-supervision responsibilities.

Delivery and professional installation

We deliver and install the tub, chiller, filtration, hoses and connections. We prepare the system for use and provide practical guidance to the nominated members of your team.

Scheduled servicing and support

We carry out a scheduled service check and clean approximately every two to three weeks. Equipment may be cleaned on site or exchanged for a professionally cleaned replacement when required.

Your gym remains responsible for daily cleaning, water testing, sanitiser management and ensuring safe use by its members. If an equipment fault occurs, contact Deep Chill promptly so that we can provide troubleshooting, repair or replacement equipment where required.

At the end of the hire period, we arrange a suitable time to disconnect and collect the equipment.

Production Hire

Cold plunge equipment for professional productions.

Suitable for photography, filming, advertising, branded content and other professional production requirements.

Send us the production brief

Tell us the date, location, production schedule, equipment required and whether the cold plunge will be used by participants or only used as part of the set.

We provide an individual quotation

We quote according to the equipment, location, access, delivery times and production schedule. Urgent bookings, extended hire, additional mileage and out-of-hours attendance may cost extra.

Delivery and installation

We deliver and install the agreed equipment at the production location. The installation area, flooring protection, access to services and final equipment position must be ready or confirmed at the agreed installation time.

Handover and collection

Once installation is complete, we hand the equipment over to the nominated production contact. We return at the agreed time to disconnect and collect everything.

The quoted setup includes up to one hour of on-site installation time unless agreed otherwise. Additional waiting or installation time caused by site access, location decisions, set preparation or other production delays may be charged separately.

Water Care

Water care depends on the type of hire.

For Home Hire, water care depends on your package: Self-Managed and Self-Managed + MyoZone customers handle day-to-day water care themselves, while Fully Managed includes a Deep Chill service visit every 14 days.

For Event Hire, we complete the fresh-water setup and provide the organiser with appropriate water-checking and safe-use guidance.

For Managed Gym Hire, Deep Chill provides scheduled servicing, while the gym remains responsible for daily cleaning, water testing, sanitiser management and member supervision between visits.

For Production Hire, the water and equipment requirements are agreed according to how the cold plunge will be used.

The specific responsibilities for each booking are confirmed before delivery and are also set out in the relevant hire terms and water-care guidance.

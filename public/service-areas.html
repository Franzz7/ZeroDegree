Service Areas

Where we deliver
cold plunge hire.

Deep Chill delivers, installs and collects cold plunge and ice bath hire across Surrey and West Sussex, from our base in Horley.

For standard home hire we cover roughly a 15-mile radius of Horley. Self-managed hire can often be arranged further out with a one-off £250 extended-area fee.

Beyond the Usual Service Area

Further out in Surrey and Sussex.

If you are outside our usual service area, self-managed hire is often still possible. A one-off £250 extended-area fee covers delivery, professional setup, handover and collection, on top of the monthly hire charge.

That regularly includes places such as Horsham, Dorking, Epsom, Guildford, Haywards Heath, Burgess Hill and Oxted. Fully Managed hire, which needs a service visit every 14 days, is limited to our usual service area.

The quickest way to find out is simply to ask. Send us your postcode and we will tell you honestly what is available where you are.

The Establishment should display any hygiene and safety information supplied by Deep Chill in a visible location near the plunge.

The Establishment is responsible for managing access to the equipment while it is under the Establishment’s control.

  • do not deliberately hyperventilate before entering
  • do not carry out underwater breath-holding exercises
  • keep their head above the water
  • do not use the plunge while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs
  • leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • follow reasonable instructions from the Establishment or Deep Chill

The Establishment is responsible for considering, through its own risk assessment, matters including:

5. Commercial and Temporary Placements

A Commercial or Temporary Placement is where Deep Chill equipment remains at the Establishment beyond a short event or activation.

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, Deep Chill will:

  • deliver and install the agreed equipment
  • explain the basic operation
  • check the initial setup
  • check the initial water levels before handover
  • provide reasonable equipment-related support

After handover, the Establishment is responsible for:

  • daily operation
  • water care
  • testing
  • cleaning
  • supervision
  • user access
  • safe use
  • reporting faults or incidents
  • following the Commercial & Event Water Care Guide

For short-term placements where the tub is emptied, cleaned and refilled daily, Deep Chill may decide that a MyoZone unit is not required. If the tub is not emptied and refilled daily, or if use is heavier than expected, the water-care arrangement may need to be reviewed.

6. Events and Short-Term Activations

An Event Arrangement is a short-term supply of equipment for an event, open day, members’ day, demonstration, promotional activation or similar activity.

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, Deep Chill will:

  • deliver the equipment
  • set it up before the event
  • carry out the initial equipment checks
  • carry out the initial agreed water checks
  • explain the basic operating and safety procedures
  • return after the event to collect the equipment

Once the equipment has been handed over for the event period, the Establishment is responsible for:

  • supervising access
  • managing queues and user flow
  • ensuring users follow the rules
  • monitoring for obvious faults, leaks or contamination
  • carrying out any agreed checks
  • stopping use where there is a concern
  • contacting Deep Chill promptly if there is a significant issue

Unless specifically agreed in writing, Deep Chill is not required to stay at the event or return during the event.

Deep Chill’s delivery, setup and initial checks do not make Deep Chill responsible for the overall management, supervision or safety of the venue or event.

8. Electricity and Equipment Safety

The chiller and any electrical equipment must only be connected to a suitable and safe electrical supply.

The Establishment must not:

  • open the chiller

  • use damaged electrical equipment
  • use unsuitable adapters
  • move electrical equipment without permission
  • use extension leads unless agreed with Deep Chill

The Establishment must stop use and contact Deep Chill if there is electrical tripping, damaged cables, smoke, burning smell, unusual electrical behaviour or any other electrical safety concern.

Once installed, the Establishment must not move, relocate, disconnect, modify, repair or alter the equipment without Deep Chill’s permission.

The equipment must only be used at the agreed location and for the agreed purpose.

If urgent action is needed to prevent injury or serious property damage, the Establishment may take reasonable steps to make the area safe and must contact Deep Chill as soon as possible.

10. Water Care

For placements and shared-use settings, the Establishment must follow the Commercial & Event Water Care Guide unless a different written arrangement is agreed.

The water-care procedure may include:

  • fresh water
  • filtration
  • bromine treatment
  • test-strip checks
  • pH checks
  • cleaning
  • filter checks
  • water changes
  • records
  • optional MyoZone use where agreed

The Establishment must not use unapproved chemicals, substitute its own treatment system, or add products to the water without Deep Chill’s agreement.

The Establishment must stop use where the water-care guide says the plunge should not be used.

11. Consumables

Unless agreed otherwise, the Establishment is responsible for providing and maintaining enough routine water-care consumables after handover, including where applicable:

  • test strips
  • bromine granules
  • bromine tablets
  • floating bromine dispenser
  • pH increaser
  • pH decreaser
  • suitable cleaning products
  • any other agreed consumables

Deep Chill may provide a starter supply where agreed, but this is not an ongoing supply commitment unless confirmed in writing.

12. User Hygiene and Safe Use

The Establishment must take reasonable steps to ensure users follow the safety and hygiene rules provided by Deep Chill.

Users should:

  • enter and exit carefully
  • keep their head above water
  • breathe normally
  • use sensible immersion times
  • leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • avoid using the plunge while dirty
  • avoid heavy oils, lotions or fake tan
  • follow instructions from the Establishment

Users must not:

  • dive or jump in
  • deliberately hyperventilate
  • practise underwater breath-holding
  • intentionally swallow the water
  • use the plunge while intoxicated
  • add products to the water
  • use the plunge if they cannot safely enter and exit

Cold water immersion may not be suitable for everyone. Deep Chill does not provide medical advice. Users who are unsure should seek appropriate medical advice.

The Establishment is responsible for setting and enforcing its own rules for children, young people and vulnerable users, including supervision, consent, safeguarding and insurance considerations.

No person under 18 should use the equipment without appropriate adult supervision and any consent required by the Establishment’s own policies.

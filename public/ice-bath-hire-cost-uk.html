How much does it cost
to hire an ice bath?

A straight answer on cold plunge hire pricing in the UK, what should be included, and the costs that sit outside the monthly figure.

Published 18 August 2026 · All guides

The short answer

Cold plunge and ice bath hire in the UK is normally charged as a monthly subscription for home use, or per setup for one-off events.

Deep Chill home hire runs from £180 a month for self-managed hire to £250 a month for fully managed hire, with a two-month minimum and no deposit. Event hire starts at £125 per setup. You can see the full breakdown on our cold plunge hire prices page.

The more useful question is not the headline number, though. It is what that number actually covers.

What should be included in the price

Before comparing two hire quotes, check whether each one includes:

  • The tub, chiller and filtration. A cold plunge without a chiller is just a tub you have to fill with ice, which gets expensive and tedious very quickly.
  • Delivery and professional setup. Some companies quote a monthly rate and then charge separately to deliver and install it.
  • Handover. Someone should show you how the equipment works and how to look after the water before they leave.
  • Collection at the end. Check this is included rather than billed later.
  • Support if something breaks. Equipment faults should be the hire company's problem, not yours.

Every Deep Chill package includes all of the above. Where packages differ is who looks after the water day to day.

Self-managed or fully managed

This is the single biggest thing that moves the price, and it is worth being honest with yourself about it.

Self-managed hire is cheaper because you handle the routine water care yourself. It is not difficult, and we hand over a clear routine on installation day, but it is a genuine ongoing task rather than something you can ignore.

Fully managed hire costs more because we visit every 14 days to clean the tub, refresh the water and check the equipment. If you know you will not keep on top of water care, this is the option that actually gets used rather than quietly abandoned after a month.

There is a middle option too: self-managed hire with an Ozone Unit, which helps support water quality between cleans while you still handle the routine.

The costs that sit outside the monthly price

Any honest answer to "what does it cost" has to include these:

  • Electricity. The chiller needs power to run. How much depends on the weather, the temperature you choose, how well insulated the tub is, how often you use it and your tariff. It is not nothing, but it is a running appliance rather than a shock.
  • Water. Refills and refreshes use water. Modest, but not zero.
  • Your time, if you go self-managed.
  • Extended-area delivery. If you are outside the usual service area, there may be a one-off fee. Ours is £250 and it covers delivery, setup, handover and collection.

Hiring versus buying

Buying a cold plunge outright means a significant upfront cost for the tub and chiller, plus everything that comes after it: installation, maintenance, repairs when the chiller develops a fault, storage if you stop using it, and eventually disposal.

Hiring spreads that into a monthly figure, and moves the risk of equipment failure onto the hire company. It also means you can stop.

That last point matters more than people expect. Plenty of enthusiasm for cold water does not survive contact with a wet February morning. Hiring for a couple of months tells you whether you are someone who will genuinely keep it up, at a fraction of the cost of finding out the expensive way.

If you already know you are committed and you have somewhere permanent to put it, buying may well work out better over several years. If you are not certain yet, hiring is the lower-risk way to find out.

What to ask any hire company

  • What exactly is included in the monthly price?
  • Is there a minimum hire period, and what notice do I need to give?
  • Is there a deposit?
  • Who is responsible if the chiller fails?
  • Who looks after the water, and what happens if I fall behind?
  • Is delivery, setup and collection included, or extra?
  • Do you cover my postcode without an additional charge?

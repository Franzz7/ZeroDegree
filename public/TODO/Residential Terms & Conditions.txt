14. Health and Safety
Cold water immersion carries risks and may not be suitable for everyone.
Users are responsible for considering whether cold water immersion is suitable for them.
Deep Chill strongly recommends seeking appropriate medical advice before using cold water immersion where a person:
has a heart condition;
has high or low blood pressure;
has breathing difficulties;
has a history of fainting;
is pregnant;
takes medication that may be relevant to cold exposure;
has a medical condition that may be affected by cold exposure; or
is unsure whether cold water immersion is suitable.
Deep Chill does not provide medical advice.
Users should:
enter the water slowly and carefully;
avoid deliberately hyperventilating before entering;
keep immersion times appropriate to their experience and the conditions;
avoid using the plunge while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs;
leave the water immediately if they feel unwell; and
warm up safely after use.
Users should stop immediately if they feel:
dizzy;
faint;
breathless;
confused;
unusually numb;
severely uncomfortable; or
otherwise unwell.
The equipment must be used responsibly.

15. User Hygiene and Contamination

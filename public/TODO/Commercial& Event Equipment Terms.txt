written monitoring records.

HSE recognises PWTAG guidance as a relevant standard for effectively managed pool water systems, while HSE's commercial-type spa-pool guidance emphasises risk assessment, operation, maintenance, water-quality testing and monitoring.

18. User Hygiene
The Establishment must take reasonable steps to communicate the hygiene rules specified by Deep Chill.

avoid use with significant open wounds;
avoid introducing food, drink or foreign substances;
keep their head above the water; and
avoid intentionally swallowing the water.
The Establishment should display any hygiene and safety information supplied by Deep Chill in a visible location near the plunge.

19. User Management and Safe Use
Cold water immersion carries risks and may not be suitable for everyone.
The Establishment is responsible for managing access to the equipment while it is under the Establishment's control.
The Establishment must take reasonable steps to ensure that users:
receive appropriate basic safety information;
enter and leave carefully;
do not dive or jump into the plunge;
do not deliberately hyperventilate before entering;
do not carry out underwater breath-holding exercises;
keep their head above the water;
do not use the plunge while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs;
leave immediately if they feel unwell; and
follow reasonable instructions from the Establishment or Deep Chill.
The Establishment is responsible for considering, through its own risk assessment, matters including:
supervision;
user access;
operating hours;
vulnerable users;
children and young people;
entry and exit;
surrounding floor conditions; and
emergency arrangements.
HSE guidance says operators should carry out suitable and sufficient assessments of risks to workers and users and select controls appropriate to the particular facility and circumstances.

have breathing difficulties;
have a history of fainting;
are pregnant;

not reopen until the required corrective action has been completed.

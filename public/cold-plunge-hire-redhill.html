Installing in Redhill

What to check before you book.

Redhill mixes Victorian and Edwardian terraces with newer estates around Earlswood, Whitebushes and Salfords. Newer properties usually pose no access problems at all. If you are in an older terrace where the only route to the garden is through the house, tell us at the enquiry stage — it is often still workable, but we would rather plan it than discover it on delivery day.

The general requirements are set out in how hire works, and we confirm everything with you before anything is booked.

Common Questions

Do you cover all of Redhill?
Yes. We cover Redhill and the surrounding RH1 area, including Merstham, Earlswood, Whitebushes, Salfords, South Nutfield and Meadvale.
Is Fully Managed hire available in Redhill?
Yes. Redhill is inside our usual service area, so all three packages are available, including Fully Managed at £250 a month with a service visit every 14 days.
Is there a delivery charge for Redhill?
No. Delivery, professional setup, handover and collection are included. The one-off £250 extended-area fee applies only outside our usual service area, and Redhill is inside it.
I live in a Victorian terrace. Can you still install?
Usually, yes. The main thing we need to check is how we get the tub to your outdoor space and whether there is a suitable power supply and outdoor tap. Send us a couple of photos with your enquiry and we will confirm before you commit to anything.

Safety note: Cold water immersion is not suitable for everyone. Please seek medical advice before use if you have any heart conditions or other underlying health concerns.

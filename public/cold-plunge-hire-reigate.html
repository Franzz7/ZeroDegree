Installing in Reigate

What to check before you book.

Reigate has a good share of period properties and larger plots, which usually means plenty of room for a cold plunge and a quiet spot to put it. Two things worth flagging when you enquire: gravel or steeply sloping driveways can affect how we bring equipment in, and a tub needs to sit on a firm, level base rather than soft lawn or loose gravel. We will go through this with you before booking.

The general requirements are set out in how hire works, and we confirm everything with you before anything is booked.

Common Questions

Do you cover all of Reigate?
Yes. We cover Reigate and the surrounding RH2 area, including Woodhatch, Meadvale, Sidlow, Doversgreen and Reigate Heath.
Is Fully Managed hire available in Reigate?
Yes. Reigate is inside our usual service area, so all three packages are available, including Fully Managed at £250 a month with a Deep Chill service visit every 14 days.
Is there a delivery charge for Reigate?
No. Reigate is inside our usual service area, so delivery, professional setup, handover and collection are all included in the monthly hire price.
My garden is on a slope. Is that a problem?
The tub itself needs a firm, level base, but that does not have to be the whole garden — a patio, decking or a level section of hardstanding is usually fine. Send photos with your enquiry and we will tell you honestly whether it will work.

Safety note: Cold water immersion is not suitable for everyone. Please seek medical advice before use if you have any heart conditions or other underlying health concerns.

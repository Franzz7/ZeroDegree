Installing in Crawley

What to check before you book.

Much of Crawley was built as a post-war new town, which tends to work in your favour: a large share of properties have private rear gardens with side or rear access, and off-street parking for the delivery van. If you are in one of the older terraces around Northgate or West Green where access is only through the house, let us know when you enquire and we will talk it through before booking.

The general requirements are set out in how hire works, and we confirm everything with you before anything is booked.

Common Questions

Do you cover all of Crawley?
Yes. We cover the whole of Crawley across the RH10 and RH11 postcodes, including Three Bridges, Maidenbower, Pound Hill, Ifield, Southgate, Tilgate, Furnace Green, Broadfield, Langley Green, Gossops Green, West Green, Northgate and Worth.
Is Fully Managed hire available in Crawley?
Yes. Crawley is inside our usual service area, so all three packages are available: Self-Managed at £180 a month, Self-Managed + Ozone Unit at £200, and Fully Managed at £250 a month with a Deep Chill service visit every 14 days.
Is there a delivery charge for Crawley?
No. The one-off £250 extended-area fee only applies to addresses outside our usual service area. Crawley is inside it, so delivery, professional setup, handover and collection are included in the monthly price.
What do I need at my Crawley property?
A stable, level outdoor space, a nearby outdoor tap, a safe outdoor power socket and clear access for delivery. We confirm all of this with you before booking, and can review photos of your space if you are not sure.

Safety note: Cold water immersion is not suitable for everyone. Please seek medical advice before use if you have any heart conditions or other underlying health concerns.

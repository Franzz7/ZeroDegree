7. Water Care

Deep Chill normally uses fresh water with filtration and regular maintenance for residential hires, without routine chemical treatment.

Between scheduled maintenance visits, the customer must follow the Residential Water Care & Safe Use Guide.

The customer should stop using the plunge and contact Deep Chill if:

  • the water is significantly cloudy
  • the water smells unusual

  • water circulation has stopped or reduced significantly
  • the chiller is not operating normally
  • there is a significant leak
  • an electrical concern is suspected
  • someone has vomited in the water
  • there has been faecal or significant blood contamination
  • an unknown chemical or substance has been added

10. Health, Safety and Permitted Users

Cold water immersion carries risks and may not be suitable for everyone.

Deep Chill does not provide medical advice.

Users should seek appropriate medical advice before using cold water immersion if they have a heart condition, high or low blood pressure, breathing difficulties, a history of fainting, are pregnant, take medication relevant to cold exposure, have another medical condition that may be affected by cold exposure, or are unsure whether cold water immersion is suitable.

Users should:

  • enter and exit carefully
  • keep their head above water
  • breathe normally
  • use sensible immersion times
  • avoid diving or jumping in
  • avoid deliberate hyperventilation or underwater breath-holding
  • avoid use while affected by alcohol or recreational drugs
  • leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • follow the Residential Water Care & Safe Use Guide

No person under 18 should use the equipment without appropriate direct adult supervision.

The customer may allow occasional personal guests to use the equipment as part of normal private home use, but must make sure they understand the basic safety and hygiene rules.

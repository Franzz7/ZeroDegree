2. The Service

The standard home-hire service includes:

  • One cold plunge tub with chiller and filtration

The equipment is for private use at the address stated in the Customer Agreement Form by the customer, their household and occasional personal guests.

Commercial use, paid sessions, public events, shared facilities, sub-hire or use at another address are not permitted under a Home Hire Agreement.

All supplied equipment remains the property of Deep Chill.

The customer must:

  • Keep the tub covered when not in use
  • Leave the circulation and chiller operating as instructed
  • Keep the chiller’s ventilation openings clear
  • Protect the equipment from theft, vandalism, animals, unsuitable weather and avoidable damage
  • Monitor the equipment, visible water condition and water flow
  • Report faults, leaks, contamination, damage or reduced circulation promptly
  • Provide reasonable access for agreed servicing and collection

6. Safe Use and Supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the equipment whenever it is being used. The customer must ensure that every permitted user understands the safety instructions.

Anyone under 18 may use the plunge only where this is permitted by the equipment manufacturer and Deep Chill, and with direct adult supervision.

Users must:

  • Enter and exit carefully
  • Keep their head above water
  • Breathe normally
  • Use sensible immersion times
  • Leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • Follow the Water Care & Safe Use Guide

Users must not:

  • Dive or jump
  • Deliberately hyperventilate
  • Hold their breath underwater
  • Intentionally swallow the water
  • Use the equipment while visibly affected by alcohol, drugs or another intoxicating substance
  • Take food, drinks or chewing gum into or onto the equipment
  • Add anything to the water
  • Use damaged or apparently unsafe equipment

Cold-water immersion may not be suitable for everyone. Anyone who is pregnant or has a heart, blood-pressure or breathing condition, a history of fainting, relevant medication or another health concern should obtain appropriate medical advice before use.

7. Weather and Electrical Safety

The equipment must not be used during strong winds, thunderstorms, heavy rain or other unsafe weather.

The customer must protect outdoor equipment with the appropriate cover as instructed, without covering or restricting the chiller’s required ventilation while it is operating.

The customer must not:

  • Open the chiller or attempt electrical repairs
  • Alter plugs, cables or electrical components
  • Use damaged equipment or unsuitable adapters
  • Use an extension lead unless Deep Chill has approved it

Stop use, disconnect the power only if it is safe to do so, and contact Deep Chill immediately if there is:

  • Repeated electrical tripping
  • A damaged plug or cable
  • Smoke or a burning smell
  • Unusual electrical behaviour
  • Water entering an electrical component

8. Water Care and Servicing

Deep Chill normally services the equipment approximately every 10–14 days. Servicing may include:

  • Inspecting and cleaning the tub and waterline
  • Checking the chiller, filter, hoses and connections
  • Checking circulation
  • Rinsing, cleaning or replacing the filter where required
  • Refreshing or replacing the water where required

The service interval does not guarantee that the water will remain suitable for a fixed number of days. The customer must follow the Water Care & Safe Use Guide and contact Deep Chill before the next scheduled visit if the water or equipment condition changes.

Stop use immediately if:

  • The water becomes cloudy, dirty or smells unusual
  • Circulation stops or reduces significantly
  • The chiller is not operating normally
  • There is a significant leak or electrical concern
  • Vomit, faecal matter, significant blood, chemicals or another contaminant enters the water
  • There is any doubt about safe continued use

Deep Chill may charge reasonable additional costs where extra cleaning, water replacement, filter replacement, repairs or visits are required because of misuse, avoidable contamination or failure to follow instructions.

The customer will not be charged for fair wear and tear, normal maintenance or a fault for which Deep Chill is responsible.

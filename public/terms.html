2. Home Hire Packages

Deep Chill offers the following private Home Hire packages:

  • Self-Managed Home Hire — £180 per month. Includes one cold-plunge tub with chiller and filtration, hoses, fittings, tub cover, delivery, professional setup, handover and collection.
  • Self-Managed Home Hire + Ozone Unit — £200 per month. Includes the Self-Managed Home Hire package plus an Ozone Unit for water-care support.
  • Fully Managed Home Hire — £250 per month. Includes one cold-plunge tub with chiller and filtration, hoses, fittings, tub cover, an Ozone Unit for water-care support, delivery, professional setup, handover, collection and a Deep Chill service visit every 14 days.

The selected package, equipment, address, start date, payment date and any special arrangements will be stated in the Customer Agreement Form.

All equipment remains the property of Deep Chill.

Self-managed packages are available within Deep Chill’s usual service area and, where agreed, in selected extended areas. Fully Managed Home Hire is available within Deep Chill’s usual service area only.

For addresses outside the usual service area, self-managed hire may be available with a one-off £250 extended-area delivery, professional setup, handover and collection fee, in addition to the monthly hire charge. This fee must be paid before delivery unless Deep Chill agrees otherwise in writing.

The Ozone Unit helps support water quality between cleans. It does not replace cleaning, filtration, water checks or the water-care routine set out in the Water Care & Safe Use Guide.

5. Ownership and Care of the Equipment

All supplied equipment remains the property of Deep Chill.

The customer must:

  • Follow Deep Chill’s instructions and the Water Care & Safe Use Guide
  • Take reasonable care of the equipment
  • Keep the tub covered when not in use
  • Leave the circulation and chiller operating as instructed
  • Keep the chiller’s ventilation openings clear
  • Protect the equipment from theft, vandalism, animals, unsuitable weather and avoidable damage
  • Monitor the equipment, visible water condition and water flow
  • Report faults, leaks, contamination, damage or reduced circulation promptly
  • Provide reasonable access for agreed servicing and collection

The customer must not move, disconnect, modify, open, repair, lend, sell, sub-hire or relocate the equipment without Deep Chill’s written permission.

The customer must not add bromine, chlorine, bleach, salts, cleaners, oils, fragrances, dyes or any other substance unless Deep Chill has specifically instructed them to do so in writing.

The customer must use the Ozone Unit only as instructed. The Ozone Unit supports water quality but does not remove the customer’s responsibility to follow the applicable water-care and safe-use instructions.

Equipment that requires securing or anchoring must not be used until it has been installed as required.

6. Safe Use and Supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the equipment whenever it is being used. The customer must ensure that every permitted user understands the safety instructions.

Anyone under 18 may use the plunge only where this is permitted by the equipment manufacturer and Deep Chill, and with direct adult supervision.

Users must:

  • Enter and exit carefully
  • Keep their head above water
  • Breathe normally
  • Use sensible immersion times
  • Leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • Follow the Water Care & Safe Use Guide

Users must not:

  • Dive or jump
  • Deliberately hyperventilate
  • Hold their breath underwater
  • Intentionally swallow the water
  • Use the equipment while visibly affected by alcohol, drugs or another intoxicating substance
  • Take food, drinks or chewing gum into or onto the equipment
  • Add anything to the water
  • Use damaged or apparently unsafe equipment

Cold-water immersion may not be suitable for everyone. Anyone who is pregnant or has a heart, blood-pressure or breathing condition, a history of fainting, relevant medication or another health concern should obtain appropriate medical advice before use.

7. Weather and Electrical Safety

The equipment must not be used during strong winds, thunderstorms, heavy rain or other unsafe weather.

The customer must protect outdoor equipment with the appropriate cover as instructed, without covering or restricting the chiller’s required ventilation while it is operating.

The customer must not:

  • Open the chiller or attempt electrical repairs
  • Alter plugs, cables or electrical components
  • Use damaged equipment or unsuitable adapters
  • Use an extension lead unless Deep Chill has approved it

Stop use, disconnect the power only if it is safe to do so, and contact Deep Chill immediately if there is:

  • Repeated electrical tripping
  • A damaged plug or cable
  • Smoke or a burning smell
  • Unusual electrical behaviour
  • Water entering an electrical component

8. Water Care and Servicing

All customers must follow the Water Care & Safe Use Guide and must stop use and contact Deep Chill if there is any concern about water quality, contamination, circulation, a leak, electrical safety or continued safe operation.

Self-Managed Home Hire

For Self-Managed Home Hire and Self-Managed Home Hire + Ozone Unit, the customer is responsible for day-to-day water care and cleaning during the hire.

This includes keeping the tub covered when not in use, removing visible debris, monitoring the water’s appearance and smell, operating the chiller and circulation as instructed, and following the water-care routine provided by Deep Chill.

The Ozone Unit may help support water quality between cleans, but it does not guarantee that water will remain suitable for use or replace cleaning, filtration or the required water-care routine.

Deep Chill is not required to carry out scheduled service visits for self-managed hire unless separately agreed in writing.

Fully Managed Home Hire

For Fully Managed Home Hire, Deep Chill normally visits every 14 days. A service visit may include:

  • inspecting and cleaning the tub and waterline;
  • refreshing or replacing water where required;
  • checking the filter, chiller, circulation, hoses and connections;
  • rinsing, cleaning or replacing the filter where required; and
  • checking for leaks, faults or other equipment concerns.

The service interval is not a guarantee that the water will remain suitable for exactly 14 days. Between visits, the customer must keep the tub covered, remove visible debris, monitor the water’s appearance and smell, and contact Deep Chill if there is any concern.

Stop Use Immediately

The customer must stop use immediately and contact Deep Chill if:

  • the water is cloudy, dirty, contaminated or smells unusual;
  • circulation stops or reduces significantly;
  • the chiller is not operating normally;
  • there is a significant leak or electrical concern;
  • a hose, fitting, cable or plug is damaged;
  • vomit, faecal matter, significant blood, unknown chemicals or another contaminant enters the water; or
  • there is any doubt about safe continued use.

Deep Chill may charge reasonable and evidenced additional costs where extra cleaning, water replacement, filter replacement, repairs or visits are required because of misuse, avoidable contamination or failure to follow instructions.

The customer will not be charged for fair wear and tear, normal maintenance or a fault for which Deep Chill is responsible.

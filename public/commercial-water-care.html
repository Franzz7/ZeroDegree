Safe Use Guide

For gyms, studios, shared facilities and commercial placements · Last updated: 09/07/2026

1. Purpose of this Guide

This guide sets out Deep Chill’s recommended day-to-day operating procedure for commercial and shared-use cold plunge equipment supplied by Deep Chill.

It is intended to help the Establishment operate and maintain the equipment consistently and safely.

The Establishment remains responsible for:

  • the operation of the plunge at its premises
  • carrying out appropriate site and activity risk assessments
  • managing users and access
  • water testing and record keeping
  • cleaning and routine water care
  • staff training and supervision
  • complying with legal and regulatory requirements applicable to its premises and activities
  • following current manufacturer instructions for the equipment and products used
  • reviewing current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and determining and implementing the controls appropriate to its operation, risk assessment and legal obligations

The Deep Chill procedure is an operating recommendation for the equipment configuration supplied.

Deep Chill does not represent that an installation has been independently certified as compliant with PWTAG or any other technical standard unless expressly confirmed in writing.

2. Equipment Used in the Deep Chill Setup

A commercial Deep Chill setup may include:

  • Deep Chill cold plunge tub
  • MyoChill chiller and filtration system
  • MyoZone ozone unit
  • Bluewater 20 g Bromine Tablets
  • adjustable floating bromine dispenser
  • Lovibond Bromine & pH Pooltester
  • supplementary test strips
  • ClearWater pH Plus
  • ClearWater pH Minus
  • suitable non-abrasive cleaning products compatible with the tub material

The MyoChill and MyoZone must be operated and maintained according to the current instructions and guidance provided by their manufacturer.

The Establishment is responsible for checking and following current manufacturer instructions, as product guidance and maintenance recommendations may change.

3. Responsible Person

The Establishment must appoint at least one responsible person for the plunge.

The responsible person should know how to:

  • operate the MyoChill
  • operate the MyoZone
  • use the Lovibond tester
  • test and record free bromine and pH
  • manage the bromine dispenser
  • adjust pH
  • carry out the cleaning procedure
  • inspect the filter and water flow
  • recognise contamination or poor water condition
  • close the plunge where necessary
  • report equipment faults to Deep Chill

Where several members of staff share responsibility, the Establishment should ensure that they follow the same procedure.

4. Recommended Daily Opening Check

Before the first user enters the plunge each day, Deep Chill recommends checking that:

  • ☐ the water is reasonably clear
  • ☐ there is no unusual or concerning smell
  • ☐ there has been no known contamination incident
  • ☐ the water level is suitable
  • ☐ the MyoChill is operating normally
  • ☐ water is circulating normally
  • ☐ hoses and fittings are secure
  • ☐ there is no significant leak
  • ☐ water flow appears normal
  • ☐ the MyoZone has been checked
  • ☐ free bromine has been tested
  • ☐ pH has been tested
  • ☐ free bromine is within the operating range adopted by the Establishment
  • ☐ pH is within the operating range adopted by the Establishment
  • ☐ the floating bromine dispenser has been removed before users enter
  • ☐ the entry and exit area is safe
  • ☐ test results have been recorded

The plunge should remain closed where there is reasonable doubt about water quality, equipment operation or user safety.

5. Deep Chill Recommended Water Operating Ranges

For the current bromine-treated Deep Chill commercial setup, Deep Chill recommends:

  • Free bromine: 4–6 mg/L
  • pH: 7.2–7.6

These recommendations are based on current commercial cold-water immersion guidance.

The Establishment remains responsible for reviewing current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and determining the operating requirements and controls appropriate to its own facility and use.

The recommended process is:

Test → record → adjust where necessary → circulate → retest → reopen when readings are acceptable.

6. Recommended Testing Frequency

Deep Chill recommends testing free bromine and pH:

  • before opening the plunge for use
  • every two hours while the plunge is open for use
  • after making a water-treatment adjustment
  • after adjusting the bromine dispenser
  • after refilling the plunge
  • following corrective water treatment
  • following a contamination response
  • whenever there is doubt about water condition

The Establishment should maintain written or electronic records of test results and corrective action.

The Establishment is responsible for reviewing current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and adopting any additional testing requirements appropriate to its operation.

7. Bromine Treatment

The current Deep Chill setup uses:

  • Bluewater 20 g Bromine Tablets
  • an adjustable floating dispenser

The tablets must remain inside the dispenser.

Do not:

  • place bromine tablets directly into the tub
  • crush or break the tablets into the water
  • add bromine granules to this system
  • mix incompatible water-treatment chemicals
  • substitute another treatment system without checking compatibility with the equipment

Deep Chill recommends that the floating dispenser is removed before users enter the plunge.

The free bromine level must be managed through testing.

Do not assume that one tablet represents one fixed dose.

If the free bromine level is too high or too low:

  • close the plunge where the reading is outside the adopted operating range
  • adjust or remove the dispenser as appropriate
  • allow the water to circulate
  • retest
  • reopen only when the reading is acceptable

8. pH Correction

Deep Chill currently recommends:

  • To raise pH: ClearWater pH Plus
  • To lower pH: ClearWater pH Minus

The Establishment should follow the current product label and instructions.

Where pH is outside the adopted operating range:

  • close the plunge
  • confirm the test result
  • calculate the required adjustment according to the product instructions and approximate tub volume
  • add the product exactly as directed
  • allow the required circulation time
  • retest
  • record the action
  • reopen when the pH is within the operating range

Do not:

  • guess chemical quantities
  • make unnecessarily large adjustments
  • mix treatment chemicals together
  • use household products as substitutes for pH-adjustment chemicals
  • reopen before the water has been retested

9. MyoZone

The MyoZone must be operated in accordance with current MyoMaster instructions.

Deep Chill’s current recommended operating procedure is:

Run MyoZone for a total of 2–4 hours per day. Where there has been heavy use, schedule part of that operating time after the heavy-use period where practical.

The Establishment should check current manufacturer instructions and adjust its operating procedure where manufacturer guidance changes.

MyoZone is supplementary and does not remove the need for:

  • testing
  • bromine management
  • filtration
  • cleaning
  • water changes
  • user hygiene

10. MyoChill and Filter Care

The MyoChill should be operated and maintained according to current manufacturer instructions and guidance.

Deep Chill recommends that the Establishment:

  • checks water flow daily
  • inspects the filter regularly
  • cleans or rinses the filter where appropriate and in accordance with current manufacturer and Deep Chill instructions
  • replaces the filter when required by current manufacturer guidance
  • replaces the filter earlier where it becomes excessively dirty, water flow reduces or filtration performance deteriorates
  • uses only compatible filters suitable for the MyoChill
  • records filter cleaning and replacement

The Establishment must refer to the current MyoMaster instructions and support guidance for filter maintenance and replacement intervals, as manufacturer recommendations may change.

Deep Chill recommends earlier inspection, cleaning or replacement where:

  • the filter becomes excessively dirty
  • water flow reduces
  • filtration performance deteriorates
  • water quality indicates that earlier attention is required

The Establishment remains responsible for reviewing current PWTAG guidance relevant to filtration and water treatment for commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and implementing any additional filter-management procedures appropriate to its commercial operation.

The Establishment must not operate the equipment without the required filter.

11. Cleaning and Water Changes

Daily Cleaning

The plunge should be cleaned daily.

Deep Chill recommends the following routine checks and cleaning steps, while the Establishment remains responsible for adopting a cleaning and disinfection procedure consistent with current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, manufacturer compatibility requirements and its own risk assessment.

At the end of the operating day:

  • inspect the tub interior and waterline
  • remove visible debris
  • remove visible body oils, grease and scum-line residue where required
  • clean the entry and exit area
  • manage pooled water
  • inspect the surrounding floor area
  • record significant water-quality or cleanliness concerns

A suitable non-abrasive cleaning product may be used to remove body oils and greasy residue.

The product must be compatible with the tub material and used in accordance with its current instructions.

It must:

  • be used sparingly
  • not be poured into the operating plunge water
  • be removed thoroughly after cleaning
  • never be mixed with water-treatment or disinfection chemicals

The Establishment remains responsible for reviewing current PWTAG guidance relevant to cleaning and disinfection for commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and adopting a cleaning and disinfection regime appropriate to its operation.

Full Drain, Clean and Refill

Deep Chill recommends a full water change and clean:

At least weekly, or sooner where water condition, contamination or heavy use requires it.

Recommended procedure:

  • Close the plunge
  • Isolate equipment as instructed
  • Drain the tub
  • Remove oils and residue using a suitable non-abrasive cleaning product compatible with the tub material
  • Rinse thoroughly
  • Disinfect the empty tub using an appropriate product and procedure selected by the Establishment in accordance with current relevant PWTAG guidance, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and the product instructions. Before using a disinfectant on Deep Chill-owned equipment, the Establishment must ensure that the product is compatible with the tub and equipment materials, using manufacturer guidance or confirmation from Deep Chill where necessary
  • Complete the required contact period
  • Rinse where required by the chosen product instructions
  • Inspect the tub, hoses and fittings
  • Inspect and clean or replace the filter where required
  • Refill with fresh mains water
  • Restart circulation and filtration
  • Restart bromine treatment
  • Operate MyoZone according to its operating schedule
  • Test free bromine and pH
  • Adjust where necessary
  • Record the water change and test results
  • Reopen when operating conditions are acceptable

12. User Hygiene and Safe Use

The Establishment should establish and communicate clear user rules.

Deep Chill recommends that users:

  • shower before entering
  • remove significant dirt
  • avoid excessive oils, lotions and heavy fake tan
  • enter slowly
  • keep their head above the water
  • breathe normally
  • use sensible immersion periods
  • leave immediately if they feel unwell
  • exit carefully

Users should not:

  • dive or jump into the plunge
  • deliberately hyperventilate before entry
  • practise underwater breath-holding
  • intentionally swallow the water
  • use the plunge while intoxicated
  • add products to the water
  • use the plunge where they cannot enter and exit safely

Users experiencing vomiting or diarrhoea should not use the plunge.

The Establishment should review and follow current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, in relation to illness-exclusion periods and other hygiene requirements.

Cold water immersion may not be suitable for everyone.

Users who are unsure of their suitability should seek appropriate medical advice.

13. Contamination and Closure

The plunge should be closed immediately following:

  • faecal contamination
  • vomiting
  • significant blood contamination
  • deliberate contamination
  • unknown chemicals entering the water
  • equipment failure affecting circulation or filtration
  • significant unexplained cloudiness or odour
  • a significant leak
  • an electrical safety concern
  • reasonable doubt about safe continued operation

The Establishment is responsible for having an appropriate contamination-response procedure and for reviewing and following current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, together with any other relevant guidance.

Do not attempt to resolve significant contamination simply by adding additional bromine.

The plunge should remain closed until the required corrective action has been completed.

14. Records and Microbiological Monitoring

The Establishment is responsible for establishing and implementing a monitoring, sampling and record-retention programme appropriate to its commercial operation, risk assessment and applicable guidance.

Deep Chill recommends recording:

  • free bromine readings
  • pH readings
  • corrective actions
  • cleaning
  • water changes
  • filter maintenance
  • equipment faults
  • contamination incidents
  • closures and reopening

The Establishment should review current PWTAG guidance relevant to commercial cold-water immersion, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, and obtain competent water-treatment or laboratory advice where necessary.

The Establishment is responsible for arranging microbiological water testing in accordance with current PWTAG guidance relevant to its commercial cold-water immersion operation, including Technical Note 71 where applicable, using an appropriately accredited laboratory.

The Establishment is responsible for reviewing test results, retaining the required records and obtaining competent advice where results indicate a water-quality concern.

Where Legionella or other water-system risk-management duties apply to the installation or premises, the Establishment is responsible for obtaining competent advice and meeting those requirements.

The starter supply is not an ongoing supply commitment.

After the starter supply has been used, the Establishment is responsible for purchasing and maintaining adequate stocks of routine consumables, including as applicable:

  • bromine tablets
  • Lovibond reagent tablets
  • pH Plus

  • cleaning cloths, brushes or sponges
  • suitable protective equipment required by product instructions or the Establishment’s risk assessment
  • other routine water-care products used by the Establishment

Filter replacement responsibility should be agreed in the commercial arrangement.
